Hello everyone,

I created the **tm-data** repository because there is not enough accessible and well-organized data about Turkmenistan, the country where I was born.

---

### Why I Created tm-data

My goal is to collect, improve, and openly share data related to Turkmenistan. This project is not limited to geographical information. I also want to create datasets about Turkmen poems, stories, novels, names, colors, cultural elements, and many other subjects.

I want this information to be easy to find and freely available to developers, researchers, writers, students, and anyone interested in Turkmenistan.

---

### An Open-Source Project for Everyone

I hope to expand **tm-data** over time by creating more repositories and datasets that everyone can freely use, improve, and contribute to.

I believe open-source knowledge should be accessible to everyone and created for the benefit of everyone. By sharing this data publicly, I hope other people will use it in their own projects and help it grow.

---

### Preserving the Turkmen Language

One of the most important reasons behind this project is to increase the amount of reliable Turkmen content available online. I also want to help preserve Turkmen, our native language, so that it is not forgotten or lost over time.

A language remains alive when people continue to speak it, write it, teach it, and make it available to future generations. **tm-data** is my contribution to that effort.

---

### This Is Only the Beginning

This repository is only the beginning. I hope it will grow into a valuable open-source resource for everyone interested in Turkmenistan, its language, history, literature, and culture.
